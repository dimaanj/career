api-gateway: how startup will work

Step 4 of the release stages, expanded for the gateway

1. Pod started

startupProbe OK (liveness)

2. Fetches openapi.yaml

from 9 services, with a connect/read timeout, retry every 30 s (was 5 min)

3. Critical ones loaded?

otp-validator, settings, user-profile, support-panel (+ prof-events). No → readiness DOWN, traffic goes to the old pod

4. Ready

pod is in the Service. Non-critical ones load on the next refresh and do not block

5. PostSync

/actuator/gateway/routes contains routes of all critical services, otherwise the hook fails

Risk

A critical service is down during the release: the new gateway does not become Ready, the old one keeps working, and the rollout hits progressDeadlineSeconds. Better than a 404, but needs an alert.

To decide

A critical service goes down after the release: its routes are removed after 15 min, and the whole gateway becomes NotReady. Options: readiness checks “loaded at least once”, or increase the removal timeout.
